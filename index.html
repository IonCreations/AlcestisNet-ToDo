Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Alcestis Net - ToDo.

How does "Share to ToDo" work?

Highlight any text or tap the Share button in Chrome, YouTube, Drive, or Files and select Alcestis Net - ToDo. The app opens with the shared title and link/file attached automatically.

How do I purchase ToDo PRO?

You can unlock ToDo PRO via Google Play In-App Purchase inside the app settings or by tapping any locked PRO feature. It is a one-time purchase that unlocks PRO features permanently for your Google account.

Is my data private and safe?

Yes! Alcestis Net - ToDo is 100% offline-first. All your tasks, folders, and settings remain on your local device. You can export JSON backups or text summaries at any time.
