👑 How do I upgrade to ToDo PRO or restore my purchase?

Unlocking ToDo PRO gives you unlimited attachments, task sorting, tag filtering, custom recurrence intervals, specific weekday pickers, and advanced folder deletion controls:

  1. Open app Settings ⚙️ or tap any locked PRO feature.
  2. Tap Upgrade to Pro 👑 to open the official Google Play Store purchase sheet.
  3. Once payment is confirmed by Google Play, your account automatically unlocks PRO permanently.
  4. Restoring Purchases: If you reinstall the app or switch devices, Google Play automatically verifies your purchase on startup—no additional code or payment needed.

⚡ How does "Share to ToDo" work?

You can create tasks instantly from Chrome, YouTube, Google Drive, or Files without opening the app first:

  • Highlight text or tap Share in any Android app.
  • Select Alcestis Net - ToDo from the share menu.
  • The app opens with your shared text as the task title and attaches any web link (🔗) or file link (📄) automatically.

🔄 How do custom recurrence rules work?

ToDo allows you to automate repeating tasks easily:

  • Basic Recurrence: Choose Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Yearly repetition with optional end dates.
  • PRO Recurrence Intervals: Set custom intervals like repeating every 2 months (Monthly-2) or every 3 weeks (Weekly-3).
  • PRO Weekday Selector: Pick specific days of the week (e.g., Mon, Wed, Fri for Weekly (M, W, F)).
  • Completing a recurring task automatically schedules the next due date based on your rule.

📁 How do I organize tasks into folders and tags?

  • Custom Folders: Create folders (e.g., Work, Personal) from the navigation drawer. Move tasks between folders at any time.
  • Custom Tags: In ToDo PRO, enter comma-separated keywords (e.g., urgent, finance) when creating or editing a task. Use 🏷️ Filter by Tag… in the drawer menu to filter by tag.

💾 How do I backup or transfer my tasks to a new phone?

All data is 100% private and stored locally on your phone. You can transfer your data easily:

  1. Open the top-right toolbar menu ⋮ and select Export Tasks.
  2. Choose JSON Backup and save the .json file to your device or Google Drive.
  3. On your new phone, open ToDo, select Import Tasks, and choose your JSON file. You can choose to Merge with existing tasks or Replace them.

🌐 How do I change the app language?

Alcestis Net - ToDo supports English, French, German, Greek, and Spanish. Open app Settings ⚙️ $\rightarrow$ App Language and pick your preferred language.
