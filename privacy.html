Privacy Policy

Alcestis Net - ToDo for Android
Effective Date: March 2026
Summary: Alcestis Net - ToDo is an offline-first task management app. Your tasks, notes, attachments, tags, and preferences stay on your local device. We do not collect, store on external servers, or sell your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

Alcestis Net - ToDo does not require user account registration, login credentials, or remote cloud servers to operate. All data you enter into the application remains stored locally on your device:

  • Task Information: Titles, descriptions, due dates, folders, recurrence rules, and tags are stored locally using Android SharedPreferences and internal app storage.
  • Attachments & Shared Links: Web URLs and local file URIs attached to tasks remain stored locally on your device.
  • App Settings: App preferences (such as language choice, completed task toggles, first day of week setting, and scroll behavior) are saved locally.

2. Use of Device Permissions

The app requests minimal Android permissions solely to perform user-initiated actions:

  • Read External Storage / File Access: Used exclusively when you choose to attach local files or photos to a task, or when you export/import backup files.
  • Share Intent Receiver (ACTION_SEND): Processes shared text, links, or files shared from external apps (e.g., Chrome, YouTube, Files) into ToDo tasks at your explicit request.

3. Google Play Billing

In-app purchases for ToDo PRO (product ID: todo_pro_unlock) are processed securely by Google Play Billing. Payment details, credit card information, and billing processing are managed exclusively by Google Play. We do not store or process your financial or payment details.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not share, sell, or rent your personal information to third parties. The app does not integrate third-party advertising networks, analytics tracking SDKs, or external user profiling services.

5. Backup Import & Export

You have full ownership of your data. The application provides local JSON backup export/import and text export options. Exported files are saved to locations selected by you on your local device.

6. Children's Privacy

Alcestis Net - ToDo complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
